# Google sign-in logo

`google-g.png` is the unmodified official Google G asset downloaded on
5 October 2026 from https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/g-logo.png.

Source and usage guidelines: https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines.
Rendered locally with `contain`, without tint, on the white Google sign-in button.
No request to Google is made to load the icon at runtime.
